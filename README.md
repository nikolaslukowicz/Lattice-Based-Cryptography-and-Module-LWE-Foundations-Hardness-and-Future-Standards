# Lattice-Based Cryptography and Module-LWE

**Foundations, Hardness, and Future Standards**

A seminar paper exploring the mathematical foundations of lattice-based cryptography and its role in post-quantum security, with a particular focus on Learning With Errors (LWE), Module-LWE, and the NIST-standardized ML-KEM.

## Topics Covered

- Mathematical foundations of lattices and computational problems (SVP, CVP)
- Learning With Errors (LWE), Ring-LWE, and Module-LWE
- Computational hardness and quantum resistance
- Worst-case to average-case reductions
- ML-KEM (FIPS 203), including key generation, encapsulation, and decapsulation
